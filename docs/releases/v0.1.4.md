Burrow Breaker gets its real look.

## Which file

| File | Flash at | When |
|---|---|---|
| `rabbitmesh-0.1.4-tdeck-update.bin` | `0x10000` | **Already on RabbitMesh:** this one. Keeps your settings, contacts and identity. Don't erase first. |
| `rabbitmesh-0.1.4-tdeck-full.bin` | `0x0` | First install, coming from any other firmware (it resets the settings; back up first). |

Flash with the [Espressif web flasher](https://espressif.github.io/esptool-js/) in Chrome or Edge: hold the trackball while switching the T-Deck on, plug it in, connect at 921600, add the file at its address, Program. New to RabbitMesh? See the [README](https://github.com/WardenOfTheKeys/rabbitmesh#readme).

## What's new

- **Burrow Breaker** (the game on Deck > GAME, `g`; it was Burrow Breach) is redrawn from new pixel art, on the whole screen: a framed title bar with the hooded rabbit, server-rack blocks in cyan, purple and amber, steel plates that crack on the first hit, the bunny-eared hoverboard, carrots falling with their trail, a streaking packet, and magenta sparks where a block breaks, over a neon city. Eight columns now, and each level sets every block's colour (level 1 is the concept art's wall). The controls, power-ups and best score are as before.
- **Built from this repository alone.** RabbitMesh no longer downloads Squatch Mesh's code to build: it's kept here, credited, with RabbitMesh's changes in their own commit. Nothing changes on the T-Deck.
