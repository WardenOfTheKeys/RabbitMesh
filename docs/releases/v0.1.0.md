The first release of RabbitMesh: mesh firmware for the LilyGo T-Deck / T-Deck Plus on MeshCore v1.18.0, with a walking, talking cyberpunk rabbit and a Deck of radio and field tools.

**New to RabbitMesh? Read the [README](https://github.com/WardenOfTheKeys/rabbitmesh#readme) first.** It covers installing, first steps and what every key does.

## Which file

| File | Flash at | When |
|---|---|---|
| `rabbitmesh-0.1.0-tdeck-full.bin` | `0x0` | First install, coming from any other firmware. It replaces everything, settings included, so back up first. |
| `rabbitmesh-0.1.0-tdeck-update.bin` | `0x10000` | Updating RabbitMesh. Keeps your settings, contacts and identity. |

Flash with the [Espressif web flasher](https://espressif.github.io/esptool-js/) in Chrome or Edge: hold the trackball while switching the T-Deck on, plug it in, connect at 921600, add the file at its address, Program. **Don't erase before an update**; if a T-Deck won't start, flash the full file at `0x0`. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lets you check the files.

## What's in it

- **The Deck** (`t` on the home screen): 14 tools, each with a shortcut letter.
- **Drive mode**: one press starts the trip and GPX track, the mesh coverage survey, the WiFi + Bluetooth wardrive (a WiGLE CSV you upload yourself) and the ALPR camera warnings; a summary when you stop.
- **Flock hunt**: a map of ALPR cameras around you from DeFlock / OpenStreetMap, warnings as you approach, and the radio listening for them. Laptops with the same radio maker are labelled as what they are, not as cameras.
- **Fox hunt**: walk down any mesh signal, one repeater, or one WiFi network, with a "which way is stronger" hint.
- **RF monitor** (with packet detail and a packet log), **spectrum analyzer**, **airtime**, **nearby radar**, **trip** (survives a restart), **MECP** emergency messages, **SOS**, **range test**, **breadcrumb trail** and an **SD card viewer**.
- **Themes**: Neon Rabbit, Red Team, Blue Team, and holiday costumes that come round by themselves.
- **The clock** sets itself from the GPS, the internet, the phone, or the mesh around you, and the time zone follows your location (North America).
- **New versions**: once a week, on WiFi, it checks this page for a newer release and tells you. It never installs anything by itself (Settings > System turns it off).

## Good to know

- **Built and tested on a computer, lightly on a T-Deck.** The tools' logic passes its host tests and the firmware runs on a T-Deck, but the newest tools haven't had much time in the field yet. Reports and fixes are welcome.
- **Each Bluetooth PIN is different**: find yours under Settings > Bluetooth.
- **Free software (GPL-3.0)**, built on Squatch Mesh and tools that started from WadaMesh. See the README's credits.
