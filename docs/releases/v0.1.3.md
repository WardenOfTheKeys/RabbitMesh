A game on the Deck, and SD cards that tell you what's wrong.

## Which file

| File | Flash at | When |
|---|---|---|
| `rabbitmesh-0.1.3-tdeck-update.bin` | `0x10000` | **Already on RabbitMesh:** this one. Keeps your settings, contacts and identity. Don't erase first. |
| `rabbitmesh-0.1.3-tdeck-full.bin` | `0x0` | First install, coming from any other firmware (it resets the settings; back up first). |

Flash with the [Espressif web flasher](https://espressif.github.io/esptool-js/) in Chrome or Edge: hold the trackball while switching the T-Deck on, plug it in, connect at 921600, add the file at its address, Program. New to RabbitMesh? See the [README](https://github.com/WardenOfTheKeys/rabbitmesh#readme).

## What's new

- **Burrow Breach** (Deck > GAME, `g`). A brick-breaker in the rabbit's style: its hoverboard bats a data packet into a firewall. Five levels, then round again faster; white ICE blocks take two hits; broken blocks sometimes drop a carrot (**W** wider board, **M** three packets, **S** slower packet, **+** a life). Roll the trackball, `a` / `d` or a finger to move, press to launch, `p` pause, `s` sound. The best score is kept. The mesh keeps running while you play, and the game pauses itself when the screen goes dark.
- **SD cards: "not FAT32" instead of "no sd card".** The T-Deck reads FAT32 only, and cards over 32 GB come as exFAT, so a card that was in looked like no card at all. The tools now say "sd card not FAT32", and the SD card tile (`c`) explains it and offers to **format the card as FAT32** right there (it erases the card). It also looks in the slot as soon as you open it, so a card you just put in shows up straight away. The README has a new [SD card](https://github.com/WardenOfTheKeys/rabbitmesh#the-sd-card) section on formatting one on Windows, Mac and Linux.
