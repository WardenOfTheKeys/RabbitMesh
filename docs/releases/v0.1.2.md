Walk down a WiFi network or Bluetooth device, straight from the wardrive.

## Which file

| File | Flash at | When |
|---|---|---|
| `rabbitmesh-0.1.2-tdeck-update.bin` | `0x10000` | **Already on RabbitMesh:** this one. Keeps your settings, contacts and identity. Don't erase first. |
| `rabbitmesh-0.1.2-tdeck-full.bin` | `0x0` | First install, coming from any other firmware (it resets the settings; back up first). |

Flash with the [Espressif web flasher](https://espressif.github.io/esptool-js/) in Chrome or Edge: hold the trackball while switching the T-Deck on, plug it in, connect at 921600, add the file at its address, Program. New to RabbitMesh? See the [README](https://github.com/WardenOfTheKeys/rabbitmesh#readme).

## What's new

- **Follow one, from the WiFi + BT wardrive.** Its menu has "follow one": what it saw in the last two minutes, strongest first. Pick a network or device and the fox hunt opens locked on it, with the signal meter, the Geiger click and, as you walk with a GPS fix, the "which way is stronger" arrow.
- **Bluetooth in the fox hunt.** A fourth mode (`m`): list the Bluetooth devices around you and follow one by its address. It only listens. Devices on a fixed address come first; a phone's random address changes every few minutes, so it can't be followed for long.
- **A clearer direction arrow** (fox hunt and flock hunt). It now points relative to the way you're walking (up = straight ahead) and says it in words: "GO AHEAD-RIGHT". Standing still, it names the compass direction and asks you to walk a few steps. With no direction yet, it says exactly why: no GPS fix, still gathering readings, how far you've walked, "turn a corner" (a straight walk can't tell left from right), or a signal too even to tell. It also stops jumping about.
- **"Waiting for a GPS fix" says what the GPS hears.** The GPS page counts the satellites the receiver hears, which climbs before it has worked out where you are; the tools need that position. While they wait they now show how many satellites it hears and how strong the best one is, with a hint ("open sky helps").
- **No more "the wardrive is using the radio".** A running WiFi + BT wardrive pauses while you follow something and carries on after.
